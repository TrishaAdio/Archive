How this archive is kept private

  • 01
    Your key is locked to one network. Once redeemed, the same key used from a different IP address is refused, even though the key itself is correct.
  • 02
    We never store your key. Only a SHA-256 hash is saved, so a database leak cannot reveal a working key.
  • 03
    Video links die in about two minutes. Every playback link is signed for your session and your IP, so a copied link stops working almost immediately.
  • 04
    No third-party tracking. Nothing on this page loads from another company's servers, so no outside party sees what you watch.
